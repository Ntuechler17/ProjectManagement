Paragraph Kapitel

So I was thinking about something while writing a plot about a team of investigation people coming back to a timeline they visited in 2011 for the 3rd time. What makes repeating such a story intereting?

I'm glad you asked. The best way to make such a story interesting is by applying rules to the cognitive construction. What do we understand by that. Put into simple words, imagine your Protagonist comingwith the confidence of a bull whose heartrace trancends heaven and earth cause he feels like he will own the town...

How do we handle him... simple we make the cognitive world adapt to his behavior pattern. Answer with too much strength? The next enocounter will adapt, reveal too much of your previous loop? Make the pattern of your enemy change. Do stupid things, receive stupid prices... No matter how confident you are in a 3rd loop, if fate hates you it will make sure to adapt accordingly so that you don't come out unharmed and I think that's what makes a perfect story even if it's repeated.
